How this list stays current

Each member file in _members/ can carry an orcid, openalex, semantic_scholar or dblp identifier. A nightly job queries each of those, merges the results by normalised title, and marks any author who is a member so their name links to their profile.

{{ data.totals.members_covered }} of {{ site.members | size }} members currently have an identifier. Adding one is a two-line change.

Correcting an entry

Indexes sometimes record venues incorrectly, and they rarely carry links to datasets, models or code. Anything you put in _data/publications_manual.yml takes precedence over the fetched version, matched on the title, so a correction survives the next sync.
