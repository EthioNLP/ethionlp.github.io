Joining as a member

For groups

Registering a lab, project or student group

A team registers its Hugging Face and GitHub organisations rather than individual models. From then on, work the team publishes that covers an Ethiopian language is listed on this site automatically, and work outside those languages is not included.

Team members do not have to be listed here first.

What we ask for

  • The team name, and a sentence on what it works on.
  • Your Hugging Face organisation, your GitHub organisation, or both.
  • A contact person, so we know who to ask.

What happens next

  • The form opens a pull request adding your team to _data/organisations.yml.
  • A maintainer reviews the entry and merges it.
  • The nightly sync reads both organisations and lists what it finds.
  • Your team appears on the ecosystem page with a live count.

Being listed

What happens after you submit the form

You supply public identifiers once; the profile is generated from them.

  1. You give us your links

    A personal site, a GitHub account, a Hugging Face account, a Google Scholar or ORCID page, whichever of those you have. One is enough; more means a fuller profile. You also pick which of the six research tracks you work on.

  2. A workflow builds your profile

    It reads those profiles, works out your name, affiliation and photo, resolves your OpenAlex author record, and opens a pull request adding a single file: _members/your-name.md.

  3. A maintainer merges it

    Usually within a day or two. You get a page at /community/your-name/.

  4. Your work is listed automatically from then on

    The nightly sync picks up your new papers from OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and DBLP, your Ethiopian-language models and datasets from Hugging Face, and your relevant repositories from GitHub. Work you publish later is listed without a further submission.

Without a GitHub account

Email your links to {{ site.email }} and a member will file it for you.

What we do with your data

The site stores only what you give us, and only what is already public. Each record is a plain text file in a public repository; you can read it, edit it or delete it with a pull request.

After you join

You will be sent two documents: the community membership rules and the research guidelines. Ask in one of the channels below if you have not received them.

Contributing

What members add

Each part of this site comes from a file in one repository. The six below are the ones members ask about most often; the contribute page lists the other ways, with the effort each takes.

Dataset or model

Datasets and models

Push it to the EthioNLP organisation on Hugging Face, or to your own account with the language tagged (am, ti, om, gez…). The nightly sync finds it and lists it on the ecosystem page. Ask in a channel for write access to the organisation.

Paper

Papers and identifiers

If your member file carries an orcid, openalex, semantic_scholar or dblp id, your papers are added automatically, including ACL Anthology entries. For records the indexes miss, add them to _data/publications_manual.yml.

Event or talk

Talks and events

The monthly seminar is for work in progress. Choose a date in the form, give a title, and a pull request is opened for you. Workshops, tutorials and hackathons use the same form.

Propose a talk
News

News items

Coverage, an award, a release, a defended thesis. Add _news/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md with a title and a date, or send it to the mailing list and someone will add it for you.

A language with no work yet

Languages with no work yet

Many of Ethiopia's languages have no corpus, no annotation guidelines and no baseline. If you speak one, say so in a channel and members will help you scope the work.
