Looking for a mentor

Requesting a mentor

Write to the mentor named on the topic, and copy the mailing list so another mentor can respond if they are at capacity. Say what you have read, what you can build, and how much time you have. Two paragraphs is enough.

If nothing listed fits, describe what you want to work on. Some mentors take students on topics that were not posted.

Write to the community

Offering to mentor

Joining as a mentor

{{ cfg.offer_note }}

Add four keys to your own file in _members/: the areas you will mentor in, the levels, how many students you can take, and a sentence on what to ask you about.

To offer a specific topic rather than general supervision, use Post a topic above.
