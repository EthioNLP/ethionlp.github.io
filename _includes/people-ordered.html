{%- comment -%} Put a list of expertise entries into the community's own order. sync_expertise.py ranks people by how much it detected, which is a measure of what a crawler found rather than anything about the community. Everywhere a person appears they should appear in the same sequence as /community/, which is the `order` field on each member file. Takes: include.people list of {slug: ...} from the expertise data Sets: ordered the matching member documents, in canonical order {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ordered = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign slugs = include.people | map: "slug" | join: "," -%} {%- assign roster = site.members | sort: "order" -%} {%- for m in roster -%} {%- assign probe = "," | append: slugs | append: "," -%} {%- assign needle = "," | append: m.slug | append: "," -%} {%- if probe contains needle -%} {%- assign ordered = ordered | push: m -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
