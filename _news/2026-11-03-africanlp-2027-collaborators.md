[Walelign Tewabe Sewunetie](/community/walelign-tewabe-sewunetie/) is
looking for collaborators on an empirical study
for the **AfricaNLP 2027** workshop, co-located with ACL/EACL.

The project asks whether conversational AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini,
Copilot) systematically overlook Ethiopian small and medium-sized
enterprises that operate in Amharic and Afaan Oromoo on informal platforms
such as Telegram and Facebook Marketplace, compared with formal,
English-first businesses.

## Four open roles

Three carry a **co-author track**; the fourth is a collaborator track.

**Native Amharic NLP researcher or speaker** — co-lead commercial query
localisation, Ge'ez orthographic validation and morpho-syntactic checking.
Evaluate model output accuracy and hallucination rates in Amharic responses.

**Native Afaan Oromoo NLP researcher or linguist** — co-lead query
localisation and prompt engineering in Qubee orthography, validate idiomatic
business terminology, assist with regional merchant directory mapping
(Adama, Bishoftu and others), and audit linguistic errors in generated
responses.

**Research assistant / empirical data curator** — compile a ground-truth
dataset of 200 to 300 verified Ethiopian SMEs across Addis Ababa and regional
commercial hubs. Verify physical storefronts and active Telegram channels,
and audit model hallucinations, including phantom businesses and invented
phone numbers.

**Digital commerce or development economics specialist** *(collaborator
track)* — work on the socio-technical analysis of informal digital commerce,
mobile money adoption including Telebirr, and the economic effects of
algorithmic gatekeeping on indigenous African businesses.

## Commitment and authorship

Dataset curation and model auditing run through the fourth quarter of 2026,
with the submission finalised in early 2027. Authorship is earned, following
the ACL contribution guidelines.

## How to apply

Send a short note with your academic background, language proficiencies and
research interests to **waleligntewabe@gmail.com**.
