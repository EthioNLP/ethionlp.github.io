*EthioLLM: Multilingual Large Language Models for Ethiopian Languages with Task
Evaluation* was presented at LREC-COLING 2024. It released multilingual models
for five Ethiopian languages, Amharic, Ge'ez, Afan Oromo, Somali and Tigrinya,
plus English, together with a benchmark for five downstream tasks.
