{%- comment -%} ── Method ───────────────────────────────────────────────────── {%- endcomment -%}

How this list was built

{{ data.method }}

A paper is kept only when its title or abstract names both an Ethiopian language and a language-technology topic, and only when the field it is filed under is one where that combination means what it appears to mean. Several Ethiopian languages share a name with an administrative zone, so an unfiltered search returns a great deal of public-health research.

Two limits worth stating. Author matching uses name, ORCID and OpenAlex identifier, so two researchers who share a name are counted as one person, and a member who publishes under a spelling this site does not know will appear here by mistake. Both are correctable: add the spelling to aliases in the member file, or open an issue.
