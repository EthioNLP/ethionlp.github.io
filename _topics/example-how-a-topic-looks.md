**This is a placeholder, not a real topic.** It is here to show the shape of a
posted topic. A real one replaces every section below with specifics.

## The gap

What is missing today, and why it has not been done already. One paragraph.
A good gap statement is checkable: someone should be able to disagree with it.

## What you would do

The actual work, in the order it would happen. Data collection, then
annotation, then a baseline, then the thing that is new. Say which part is the
contribution and which part is groundwork.

## What finished looks like

What exists at the end that did not exist at the start — a dataset, a model, a
measurement, a paper — and how you would know it worked.

## What you need to know first

The prerequisites, honestly stated. It is better to lose a student at this
paragraph than in month four.
